Type: Mammals

Group: Big Cats

Habitat: African savannas, grasslands, open woodland

Diet: Carnivore (meat eaters)

Average Lifespan: 10-14 years in wild, longer in zoo

Size: 120-250 kg

Fun Fact: A lion's mane can make it look larger and may signal age and health.

Basic Information

  • Lions live in social groups called prides.
  • A pride usually contains females, cubs, and a few males.
  • Females usually do most hunting.

Adaptations

  • Strong night vision.
  • Sharp retractable claws.
  • Roaring helps defend territory.

Type: Insects

Group: Beetles

Habitat: Forest edges,farms, gardens, grasslands and woodland

Diet: Carnivore (insect-eater)

Average Lifespan: 1-2 years in average in wild but 1-3 years in zoo

Size: about 0.02 g

Fun Fact: A single ladybug can eat thousands of aphids during its lifetime.

Basic Information

  • Ladybugs are actually beetles, not true bugs.
  • Their bright red/orange colors act as a warning to predators.
  • They have 6 legs and 2 pairs of wings (hard outer wing covers and flying wings underneath).

Adaptations

  • The hard outer shell protects the soft flying wings underneath.
  • When threatened, some ladybugs release a yellow fluid from their leg joints with an unpleasant smell and taste.
