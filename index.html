Type: Mammals

Group: Big Cats

Habitat: African savannas, grasslands, open woodland

Diet: Carnivore (meat eaters)

Average Lifespan: 10-14 years in wild, longer in zoo

Size: 120-250 kg

Fun Fact: A lion's mane can make it look larger and may signal age and health.

Basic Information

  • Lions live in social groups called prides.
  • A pride usually contains females, cubs, and a few males.
  • Females usually do most hunting.

Adaptations

  • Strong night vision.
  • Sharp retractable claws.
  • Roaring helps defend territory.

Type: Mammals

Habitat: Savannas and forests

Diet: Herbivore

Average Lifespan:Around 60-70 years

Size: 120-250 kg

Fun Fact: Elephants communicate through low-frequency sounds over long distances.

Basic Information

  • Largest land animal.
  • Lives in family groups led by older females.

Adaptations

  • Trunk used for breathing, grabbing food, drinking.
  • Large ears help cool body temperature.

Type: Birds

Habitat:Antarctic regions

Diet: Fish ,squid and krill

Average Lifespan: Around 15-20 years

Size: 120-250 kg

Fun Fact: Emperor penguin fathers help protect eggs during harsh winter conditions.

Basic Information

  • Penguins cannot fly but are excellent swimmers.
  • They live in large colonies.

Adaptations

  • Dense waterproof feathers.
  • Thick fat layer for insulation.
